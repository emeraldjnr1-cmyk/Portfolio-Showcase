02 · Your problem

Four channels, made one asset at a time.

"The work should support a cohesive visual presence across these marketing channels."

Your post, Behance, November 2026
Pain 01

The same brand, a different look per channel

You listed "Klaviyo email campaigns, social media posts, website design, and Meta ad creative". When those are designed separately, type, colour and buttons drift. A shopper taps an ad, lands on the site, then opens the email, and meets three slightly different brands.

Cost today: doubt at each step of a $288 purchase, which is a considered buy, not an impulse one.
Pain 02

Every campaign starts from a blank file

You asked for "Klaviyo email campaign templates and email graphics". Without templates, each drop or sale needs a new email, new posts, new ads and a new web banner, all designed from zero.

Cost today: days of design before each send, in a season that does not wait.
Pain 03

Ads that fit one placement and test nothing

"Meta advertising creative" has to work in Feed, Stories and Reels. One square image gets cropped in the vertical placements, and one message gives Meta nothing to compare it with.

Cost today: ad spend on creative that is cropped, with no second angle to learn from.
Pain 04

Finished files nobody on your side can edit

Flat exports look fine on delivery day. Then a price changes or a new bag launches, and every update goes back to a designer, because the email is one image and the ad has no source file.

Cost today: a designer invoice for every small change, and a wait each time.

05 · How the work runs

Three steps, 12 working days.

01

Days 1 to 4 · the system and the first proof

The design system is set, then proved on a real email, two posts and two ads before the volume work starts.

  • Skeyo design system in Figma: type, colour, buttons, badges, product cards, photo treatment
  • The Klaviyo campaign template, built from editable blocks
  • Two social posts and two ad concepts, as direction for the rest
  • One round of changes, then your approval. Milestone 1 is released here.
02

Days 5 to 9 · email, social and ads in full

With the direction approved, the rest of the email, social and ad set is produced from the system.

  • Product launch template, and an abandoned cart or welcome flow template (your pick)
  • The remaining social posts, to 12 in total
  • 8 Meta ad creatives in 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16, with 2 copy angles each
  • Emails checked in Gmail and Apple Mail, on desktop and phone, before you see them
03

Days 10 to 12 · website layouts and handover

The website pieces are designed last, so they match what the ads and emails now promise.

  • Homepage hero and collection page layouts, at desktop and phone width
  • Web banners sized for your theme's sections
  • The ad template kit, with a one-page guide for your team
  • Figma source, exports and Klaviyo templates handed over. Milestone 2 is released here.

What I need from you: your logo files, product and lifestyle photography, and access to Klaviyo as a user you can remove afterwards. A photo shoot and writing long-form copy are not part of this scope: I work from your existing photos and the voice already on your site.
