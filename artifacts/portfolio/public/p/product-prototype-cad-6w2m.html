02 · Your problem

A prototype only moves as fast as its files allow.

"I need editable files and files ready for 3D printing so we can print, test, and revise the parts."

Your job post, Cad Crowd, November 2026
Pain 01

A model you cannot really edit

Sketches turned into a nice looking shape with no clean feature history. The first time a test print shows a clip is 2 mm short, someone has to remodel instead of changing one number.

Cost today: every revision starts from zero, and revising is the whole reason you print to test.
Pain 02

Files that look right and print wrong

Walls too thin to print, fits with no clearance, holes that come out small, overhangs that sag. On screen the part is perfect. On the printer it fails.

Cost today: printer hours and material, plus days lost finding out the file was the problem, not the idea.
Pain 03

Losing track of versions

Print, test, revise, repeat. By round three nobody is sure which STL is on the printer, or why a size changed between the last two.

Cost today: testing the wrong version and making the same decision twice.
Pain 04

Sharing the idea before it is protected

"An NDA is required before I share sketches, dimensions, or design details." And you are moving between Canada and Indonesia while you shortlist 29 applicants.

Cost today: a slow, careful start before a single part is modelled.

Each part is prepared for your printer, so the first print tests the design, not the file.
