02 · Your problem

A logo is easy. A system is the job.

"Each logo in 5 color treatments: full color, all-black, all-white, reversed, grayscale"

Your post, Branding Kit Design, Behance
Pain 01

One missing version and a supplier improvises

You asked for "All deliverables in 5 formats". A shoe brand sends its logo to box printers, label makers, sole and insole suppliers and shop sign makers. Each one needs a different file. If the right one is not in the folder, they stretch a JPG or redraw the mark themselves.

Cost today: a logo that looks slightly different on every surface, and reprints when it comes out wrong.
Pain 02

The school line can drift into a second brand

You want a "Sub-brand lockup for the school shoe line, consistent with the master system". If the school logo is drawn as its own thing, parents and retailers see two unrelated brands, and the trust built by one does not carry to the other.

Cost today: two brands to build on the shelf instead of one.
Pain 03

Without written rules, everyone guesses

Your list includes "clear space, minimum size" and "do's and don'ts". Without them, a distributor puts the logo on a busy photo, a printer shrinks it until the tagline fills in, and someone recolours it to match a banner.

Cost today: your time checking every artwork by hand, or mistakes that reach the market.
Pain 04

The right file is hard to find

You asked for an "Organized folder structure with clear file naming". A system this size is 280 files. If they arrive as one long list with names like final_2, the wrong one goes to the printer.

Cost today: minutes lost on every request, and the risk of printing from a low-resolution file.
