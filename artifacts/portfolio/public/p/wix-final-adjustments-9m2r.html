02 · Your problem

The last few pixels are the ones people notice.

"The site is fully designed and built. I need final adjustments to text and button alignment, spacing and positioning, and desktop and mobile optimisation. No redesign."

Your brief, Behance, November 2026
Pain 01

Text and buttons that sit slightly off

A button a few pixels lower than its neighbour, a heading that does not line up with the text under it. Each one is small, together they make a page feel unfinished.

Cost today: a finished site that looks 95% done, which visitors read as less careful than it is.
Pain 02

Spacing that changes from section to section

Big gaps in one place, cramped blocks in the next. Without one spacing rhythm, the eye cannot settle and the page feels busier than the design you made.

Cost today: your design does not get the credit it deserves.
Pain 03

The phone version drifts

Wix keeps the mobile layout separate, so a fix on desktop does not always carry over. Elements overlap, text runs to the edge, buttons end up different sizes.

Cost today: the screen many visitors see first is the one that looks least finished.
Pain 04

Hiring help that starts "improving" things

You asked for adjustments, not a redesign. A helper who moves sections, swaps fonts or adds ideas turns a small job into a new problem.

Cost today: time spent undoing changes you never asked for.
