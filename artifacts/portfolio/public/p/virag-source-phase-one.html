02 · Your problem

Every euro has to prove itself.

"I want to start with a limited commitment and make further decisions based on actual results. The goal is to attract relevant enquiries and turn them into paying clients."

Virág, Virag Source

You are a young business with a limited budget, growing in Hungary first. When you ran ads yourself, one or two enquiries came in shortly after launch, so the demand is there. Here is what stands between you and a steady flow of paying clients.

Pain 01

A wrong first move is expensive

With a limited budget, spreading it across Facebook, Instagram, Google and content at once means each channel gets too little to show a result. You spend, and still don't know what works.

What it costs: months of spend with no answer at the end.
Pain 02

Visitors who aren't ready leave without a trace

Every page on viragsource.com leads to one quote form. Someone with a product idea who isn't ready to request a formal quote has nothing smaller to say yes to, and there are no client results or reviews yet to build their trust.

What it costs: the early-stage sellers who become your best long-term clients.
Pain 03

No proof of which spend brings clients

Your Google tag and Meta pixel are installed, but what you need is an answer to one question: which search, ad or page produced each enquiry, and what did it cost?

What it costs: every budget decision is a guess.
Pain 04

Expanding before you know what converts

You plan to grow internationally. Taking an untested message into new countries multiplies the cost of getting it wrong.

What it costs: the same mistake, paid for in several languages.

05 · What's included

Step by step, exactly what happens.

1

Before launch: build and go live

Everything is set up in your own accounts, and the campaign goes live as soon as you approve the page and ads. If you still have access to the ads you ran before, I start from what brought those first enquiries.

  • Hungarian keyword research: what sellers type when they look for a China sourcing partner
  • The free cost-estimate page on viragsource.com, with a short form and a WhatsApp button
  • Your Google tag and Meta pixel connected to the form, so every request is counted with its source
  • A Google Search campaign in Hungarian, with 6 ad texts for your approval
2

14 days from launch: run, cut, improve

The campaign is checked every day. Spend moves to what produces requests and away from what doesn't.

  • Daily checks: searches that waste budget are blocked, winning ones get more
  • A ready-to-send follow-up message for every new request, so none go cold
  • A short update on day 7: spend, visits, requests, cost per request, and what changes next
3

Day 14: the review

A review with the full numbers and a clear recommendation: continue, adjust, or stop. You make the call with the data in front of you.

06 · How we decide

Three possible answers on day 14.

The targets below are a starting point. Once you tell me roughly what one new client is worth to you in profit, I set them to your real numbers before we start.

Working
4 or more real requests at under €45 each

We continue: more budget on the searches that work, then Facebook and Instagram added in the next stage.

Not yet
Requests are coming in, but each one costs too much

We change the offer or the searches before any budget is added.

Stop
Not enough real requests to justify more spend

You stop with nothing further to pay, and you keep everything that was built.
