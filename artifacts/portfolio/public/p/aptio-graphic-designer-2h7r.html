02 · Your problem

Several brands, every channel, one standard.

"Branding, fonts, website design, email marketing, social media content, and overall creative direction for multiple brands."

Your post, Behance, 24 November 2026
Pain 01

The same brand drifts from channel to channel

You want designs that "keep consistent across platforms". When emails, ads, posts and website sections are made at different times, fonts, colours and spacing slowly stop matching, and with multiple brands the drift multiplies.

Cost today: a brand that looks different in every inbox and feed, and fixes after the client notices.
Pain 02

Every request has to be explained in full

You are looking for someone who works "without needing every small detail explained". Without written rules per brand, each brief has to spell out the font, the colours and the layout again.

Cost today: your team's time writing long briefs and checking work that should already be on brand.
Pain 03

Revisions take longer than they should

You need the "ability to take feedback and revise designs quickly". When each size of an ad or banner is a separate file, one change to a headline or a colour means redoing every version by hand.

Cost today: campaign assets waiting on rework while the launch date gets closer.
Pain 04

Website mockups that change on the way to live

You want "website design improvements and mockups" with "conversion-focused design". A static mockup gets read differently by whoever builds it, and the section that goes live is not quite the one that was signed off.

Cost today: a second round of fixes on the live page, or sections waiting for developer time.

05 · How the work runs

The first 30, 60 and 90 days in the role.

Live requests start in week one. The kits are built alongside the real work, not instead of it, starting with the brands that have the most going on.

30

Days 1 to 30 · audit, first kits, live work

I join your ClickUp, read any brand guidelines you already have, and look at each brand's live website, recent emails, socials and ads.

  • Consistency audit of every brand, with a one-page fix list each (week one)
  • Brand kits for the two busiest brands: type, colour, layout rules, logo use
  • Live requests from your ClickUp queue from the first week
  • Intake template in ClickUp: brand, channel, size, copy, deadline
60

Days 31 to 60 · every brand kitted, templates for every channel

The rest of the brands get their kits, and each kit gets a template library so new requests start from a ready layout.

  • Kits for the remaining brands, signed off by your team one by one
  • Templates per channel: email banner, social post and carousel, story, ad sizes, website section
  • Short-form edit presets in CapCut: caption style, intro and outro per brand
  • Canva versions of the templates your team wants to edit without me
90

Days 61 to 90 · website and conversion work

With the kits in place, the time goes into the pages and campaigns that matter most for each brand.

  • Website section library per brand, dev-ready in Figma (built in code on Option 03)
  • Ad and email variants designed for your team to test, one change at a time
  • Monthly design review: what shipped per brand, what drifted, what to fix next
  • Kit handbook so anyone on your team can use the kits without me
