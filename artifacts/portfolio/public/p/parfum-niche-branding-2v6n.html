02 · Your problem · le problème

Niche perfume is seen long before it is smelled.

"A strong and cohesive identity that raises perceived value and creates a luxurious experience, from the first impression to the unboxing."

Your brief, Behance, November 2026
Pain 01

Buyers judge the bottle before they can smell it

Online, nobody can test a niche scent. The bottle, the label and the photo are the whole first impression, and they set the price a buyer believes the perfume is worth.

Cost today: nothing finished to show on a pre-order page, to a stockist or to a bottle supplier.
Pain 02

A big brief on a small budget gets done thinly

Logo, bottle, labels, boxes and unboxing all at once, for under $100, means each piece gets minutes, not hours. Thin work looks generic, and generic is the opposite of niche.

Cost today: a cheaper look than the scent deserves, which lowers the perceived value you asked to raise.
Pain 03

Packaging is expensive to undo

Foil plates, rigid boxes and printed labels are ordered in minimums. If the name, colours or type change after that order, the stock and the plates are wasted.

Cost today: a reprint risk on every packaging order placed before the identity is fixed.
Pain 04

Flat mockups do not show luxury

Glass, brass, foil and soft paper only read as expensive in real light. A template mockup with a logo pasted on looks like every other new brand on Instagram.

Cost today: no image that makes the materials feel real, so the finishes you choose never get seen.

05 · How the work runs · les étapes

Three milestones, in the order that holds.

01

Direction · days 1 to 3

You send the brand name, the first scent, three words for the feeling, and any bottles or houses you admire. From that I build a short mood board and two wordmark directions.

  • One-page mood board: colours, papers, finishes, references
  • Two wordmark directions, set in the label context
  • You pick one; it is refined, with the palette and type pairing fixed
  • Sign-off here before the label is designed
02

Label · days 4 to 7

The label is designed to the real size of your bottle. If you have picked a stock bottle, send its name or measurements. If not, I design to a standard 50 ml flacon and adjust once you choose.

  • One label for one scent: name, scent name, volume, required small print
  • Print-ready PDF with bleed, plus SVG and PNG
  • Notes on paper and finish to ask your label printer about
03

Bottle render · days 8 to 12

I model the bottle in Blender, apply the label, set up glass, liquid, cap and collar materials, and light it like a studio product shot. You see a draft frame before the final render.

  • Hero render at 3000 px, plus a second camera angle
  • Neutral background, plus a transparent PNG version
  • The .blend scene file, so later scents reuse the same bottle and light
