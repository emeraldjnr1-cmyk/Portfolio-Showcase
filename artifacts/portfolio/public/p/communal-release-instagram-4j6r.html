02 · Your problem

A clear direction, and no posts built from it yet.

"The visuals should support clear, engaging social-media communication for my services and audience."

Your post, Behance

"Embrace imperfection, resist sanitization, invite curiosity and make room for the full spectrum of human experience."

Communal Release Brand Identity, section 10, creative philosophy
Pain 01

The direction is written down, the graphics are not

Your document names the look precisely: "countercultural spirituality meets analog editorial design, underground music culture, and experimental visual art". It also names what to avoid, starting with "generic beige minimalist wellness branding". A designer who skims it and opens a stock wellness template gives you exactly that.

Cost today: 21 pages of direction and nothing to post from them.
Pain 02

Lo-fi texture can bury the message on a phone

You want "visible film grain and paper textures", "halftone printing and dithering" and "photocopy degradation". Your document also says activist design must "prioritize communication over decoration". On a small screen, grain laid over small type does the opposite: the post looks right and nobody can read it.

Cost today: a post people scroll past because the words do not land in two seconds.
Pain 03

Harm reduction posts sit close to Instagram's drug rules

Meta's published rules bar content that sells or promotes non-medical drugs, or gives instructions for using them. Harm reduction education sits near that line, and review is partly automated, so an image or a phrase that looks like promotion can get a post removed or shown to fewer people. Nobody outside Meta controls that outcome.

Cost today: your most useful posts carry the most risk of being limited.
Pain 04

One-off graphics mean coming back to a designer every time

You asked for "editable source files". Your document asks for three modes that "coexist within one brand through consistent typography, image treatments and a shared visual vocabulary, without every piece of content needing to look identical". A folder of finished images cannot do that. A system can.

Cost today: every new workshop, reminder or carousel is a new design job.

Safe-posting checklistFREE ON OPTION 02
Communal Release1 page · before you post

Does it read as education?

  1. Image: no substances, paraphernalia or use pictured.
  2. Words on the image: checked like the caption, because both get read.
  3. Wording: informs and cares. No prices, offers, or where to get anything.
  4. First caption line: states the purpose, education and care.
  5. Sources: listed on the last slide.
  6. Alt text: written, plain and descriptive.
  7. Original file: saved, so a removed post can be appealed or revised.
