02 · Your problem

A luxury wall, on an outlet budget.

"We are looking for a professional creative logo designer to develop luxury advertising for our jewellery department, similar to the attached reference photo."

Your post, Behance, November 2026
Pain 01

Model photographs normally mean a photo shoot

You asked for "attractive jewellery model photographs". Done the usual way, that is a photographer, a model, a studio and a day of work for four collections, which costs far more than the budget on this job.

Cost today: a jewellery wall without model imagery, or a shoot quote several times your budget.
Pain 02

Artwork that looks fine on screen goes soft on a wall

You need "high-resolution artwork for large shop wall banners". A design made at screen size and stretched to two or three metres prints blurred, and RGB gold turns dull or green in CMYK.

Cost today: a reprint, and the days the wall stays empty while you wait for it.
Pain 03

Four collections can end up looking like four shops

"Necklace, earrings, bracelets and rings collection designs" are four separate pieces of artwork. Made one at a time, the backgrounds, type and logo position drift, and the department stops reading as one.

Cost today: a wall that looks assembled, not designed, next to the jewellery it should be lifting.
Pain 04

Product photos show every flaw at wall size

You asked for "professional photo retouching". Dust, fingerprints, shop-light reflections and colour casts are invisible on a phone and obvious at one metre wide, right under your logo.

Cost today: gold that looks flat and stones that look grey on the largest advert in the store.

05 · How the work runs

Three steps, about a week.

Timing starts when I have your logo file, your product photos, the reference photo and each wall size in cm. The reference photo is attached to your post where I cannot open it, so please send it in your reply.

01

Days 1 to 2 · the look and the first banner

The necklace banner is designed first, because it sets the look for the other three.

  • Photo check: which product photos hold up at wall size, and which need a sharper retake
  • The look: background, gold tone, type and logo position, matched to your reference photo
  • Necklace banner at your wall size for you to approve, with two rounds of changes
02

Days 3 to 5 · earrings, bracelets and rings

With the look approved, the other three banners are built from the same master file.

  • Retouching of the product photos for each collection
  • Model imagery chosen and licensed per collection, with your piece placed and blended
  • All four banners sent side by side, as they will hang, with one round of changes on the set
03

Days 6 to 7 · print files and the social set

Final files are exported to your printer's spec and the campaign is resized for Instagram.

  • Print files: CMYK PDF and TIFF at full size with bleed, checked at 100% zoom
  • Print spec sheet for each banner, to hand to your printer
  • Instagram post and story for each of the four collections
