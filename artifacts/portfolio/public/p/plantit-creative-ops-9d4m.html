02 · Your problem

Two apps, one gap, and feedback falls in it.

"Making sure every client message gets a response, every piece of feedback becomes a clear task, and every deliverable goes out on time."

Your job post, Behance, 25 November 2026
Pain 01

Client messages sitting in busy WhatsApp chats

With several clients each in their own chat, a message sent mid-afternoon can sit unseen under newer ones. The client does not know it was read, so they chase, or worse, they stop asking.

Cost today: clients who feel ignored, even when the team is working hard on their content.
Pain 02

Feedback retyped between WhatsApp and Google Chat

A voice note or a long message from the client has to be turned into something an editor can act on. When it is forwarded loosely or summed up from memory, a detail goes missing and the edit comes back wrong.

Cost today: extra revision rounds, editor hours spent twice, and a client who has to repeat themselves.
Pain 03

Deliveries that slip quietly

You asked for someone to make sure editors mark work complete on time and to follow up on upcoming and overdue deliveries. Without a clear due date per task, late work is found when the client asks for it.

Cost today: missed dates the client sees first, and an apology instead of a delivery.
Pain 04

Quota trackers and blockers spotted late

Each client has a monthly quota. If the tracker is updated in batches, a client who is behind pace on day 18 only shows up on day 29, when there is no time left to fix it.

Cost today: month-end surprises: under-delivered quotas and a rushed last week for the editors.
