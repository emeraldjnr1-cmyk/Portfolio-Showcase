02 · Your problem

Five formats, several vendors, one brand.

"Prepare print-ready designs with accurate dimensions and dielines. Maintain consistent brand identity across all products."

Your post, Behance, November 2026
Pain 01

Every format pulls the brand a different way

You want to "maintain consistent brand identity across all products". A tube, a dropper bottle, a jar and a carton all have different print areas. Designed one at a time, the logo size, type and spacing drift.

Cost today: a range that reads as four brands when the products sit side by side.
Pain 02

Artwork that does not fit the real pack

You need "accurate dimensions and dielines". Artwork drawn on a guessed template has text across a fold, a logo in the tube crimp, or no bleed, and the printer sends the file back.

Cost today: days lost to file corrections, or a print run with a mistake on it.
Pain 03

You only see the pack once it is printed

You asked for "3D product mockups and packaging visualisations". Without them, the first time you see the range together is on the printer's proof, when changes are slow.

Cost today: decisions made on flat artwork, and nothing to show buyers before stock arrives.
Pain 04

You carry every question between vendors

You want someone to "coordinate with manufacturers and printing vendors". Each vendor asks about colour, finishes and file format, and every question goes through you.

Cost today: your time spent relaying file questions between the designer and the printer.
