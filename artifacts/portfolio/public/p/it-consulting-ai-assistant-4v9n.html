02 · Your problem

Senior people doing front desk work.

"Our consultants spend too much time answering repeat questions, qualifying inbound leads and coordinating meetings."

Your job post, Upwork, November 2026
Pain 01

The same questions, answered by hand

What services you offer, whether you work with companies of a certain size, how onboarding works. Every answer comes from a consultant who could be billing client work instead.

Cost today: consultant hours every week spent on answers that already exist in your documents.
Pain 02

Leads qualified on the first meeting, then booked by back-and-forth

Needs, company size, current systems and budget are only learned once someone sits down with the prospect, and finding a time takes several messages before that.

Cost today: slow replies to new prospects and meetings spent on leads that were never a fit.
Pain 03

An AI that guesses in front of an enterprise prospect

You said it yourself: it must stay on brand, never invent pricing or promises, and say so when it does not know. One made-up figure or commitment in front of a large prospect does lasting damage.

Cost today: the reason most firms hold back from putting AI in front of clients at all.
Pain 04

Knowledge spread across past proposals, with client data to protect

Proposals, templates, case studies and SOPs exist, but finding the right one is slow. Any assistant over them has to keep each client's data strictly separate and confidential.

Cost today: proposals and follow-ups written from scratch when your best past work is already on file.

05 · How the work runs

Fastest win first, voice last.

One Claude core with tools serves all three surfaces, so the rules you approve once apply in chat, on the phone and in the internal assistant. Claude Code builds and tests it, and every stage ships with its own automated tests.

The setup

One core, three surfaces, one dashboard.

  • Claude API core with tool use and structured outputs: look up a client, read a calendar, create a ticket, hand over
  • Supabase (Postgres) for leads, conversations, transcripts and documents, with row-level security for client separation
  • Retrieval with source grounding: answers cite the document they came from, or the assistant says it does not know
  • Idempotency keys on every booking, message and ticket, so a retry never doubles anything
  • CI/CD deploys, with keys and call recordings kept in secure storage, never in code

02

Existing client support and ticketing

The assistant recognises existing clients and works with your ticketing or PSA tool, whichever of Jira, Freshdesk or ConnectWise you use.

  • Ticket status and project updates for recognised clients
  • Create and update tickets from the conversation
  • Technical or urgent issues routed straight to the right consultant in Slack or Teams
03

Internal knowledge assistant

A private chat for your team over past proposals, docs, templates, case studies and SOPs.

  • Answers with source references to the exact document
  • Drafts proposals, meeting summaries and follow-up mails
  • Role-based access and strict per-client separation at the database level
04

Voice agent, inbound and outbound

Last, because it has the most moving parts. Built as Twilio or Vapi for the phone line, an ElevenLabs voice, and the same Claude core behind it.

  • Answers calls, captures details, qualifies, books and reschedules
  • Barge-in handling: it stops talking when the caller interrupts
  • Warm transfer to a consultant on request, with the summary sent first
  • Calling-hour limits on outbound, failure alerts and full logs

Security is designed to fit GDPR and ISO 27001 controls: access roles, full logs, encryption, per-client data separation and retention rules for transcripts and recordings. It is not a certification; your own compliance lead confirms the final setup.
