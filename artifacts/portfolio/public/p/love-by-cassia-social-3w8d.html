02 · Your problem

The brand is ready. Nobody owns the feed.

"Ideally, I'd love to hand over the creative reins to someone with a strong creative eye who can bring their own ideas to the table while taking ownership of the brand's social media presence, engagement and growth."

Your post on Behance, Social Media Management for a Decor Brand
Pain 01

Every part of social sits with you

You listed "strategy, creative direction, content planning, day-to-day management and growth" as one role because, today, it has no owner but you. It competes with running the brand itself.

Cost today: your hours, and a feed that moves only when you have time.
Pain 02

A photographer on the ground, with no director

You are finding a photographer and videographer in Nairobi and need someone "coordinating remotely with a photographer on the ground". Without a shot list, a shoot day produces attractive pictures that do not fit the posts you need.

Cost today: a shoot fee spent on images that cover a week, not a month.
Pain 03

The ideas still have to come from you

You want someone to "proactively develop fresh ideas, create mood boards, conceptualise shoots, develop creative content formats". A manager who waits for your brief gives you a second job: briefing the manager.

Cost today: the creative reins never actually leave your hands.
Pain 04

Engagement and growth are unowned

You named "engagement and growth" twice. Comments and DMs on a décor account are often people asking about a piece, and nobody today reports which posts bring them in.

Cost today: slow replies to interested buyers, and next month planned on a guess.
