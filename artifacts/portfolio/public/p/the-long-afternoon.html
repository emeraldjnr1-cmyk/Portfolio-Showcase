Story and moodboard

Deliverable 1 of 7

The collection is named for the hours between lunch and dinner on a Mediterranean terrace, when nobody is performing. The clothes are made for exactly that: a shirt you do not tuck, a trouser that holds a pleat without a crease, a dress that moves with the wind off the water.

Every piece follows three rules. Fabric first: the linen does the talking, so there is no visible branding anywhere. Relaxed, never sloppy: dropped shoulders sit on a clean shoulder line, wide legs are balanced by a fitted rise. Detail at touching distance: concealed plackets, horn and corozo buttons, French seams, and embroidery no larger than a thumbnail.

The men's and women's lines share one language. The same camp collar appears on his shirt and her relaxed shirt. The same 185 gsm trouser cloth is cut as a pleated taper for him and a wide leg for her. Colourways are shared across both, so the collection merchandises as one rail, not two.

References: Slim Aarons' Riviera, the cream-and-olive interiors of 1960s Capri hotels, the sun-faded limestone of Puglia, and the way old linen softens after its tenth wash.

The collection

Deliverables 3 and 4 of 7 · Sketches, technical flats, specs

Men

5 core pieces shown

The Camp Shirt
L-145 Shirting. One-piece camp collar, concealed front placket, single patch pocket, side vents. Corozo 18L buttons.
The Unstructured Blazer
L-230 Jacketing. Unlined, no canvas, no pad. Soft notch lapel, two-button, patch pockets, welt chest pocket. Half-lined sleeve for slip.
The Pleated Trouser
L-185 Trouser. Double forward pleat, tapered leg, side-adjuster waistband, slant pockets, 4 cm turn-up. No belt loops on the tailoring colourways.
The Drawstring Short
L-185 Trouser. Elasticated back, flat front with single pleat, self-fabric drawstring, 18 cm inseam. Pairs with the camp shirt as a set.
The Overshirt
L-145 Shirting or L-160 Chambray. Band collar, two flap pockets, hidden placket, drop shoulder. Worn open as a light jacket.

Women

5 core pieces shown

The Column Dress
L-120 Voile. Bias-cut column, self straps, bust darts, side slit to knee. Fully French-seamed so it needs no lining.
The Relaxed Shirt
L-120 Voile. Oversized through the body, dropped shoulder, three-quarter sleeve, point collar. Cut to wear tucked at the front only.
The Wide Trouser
L-185 Trouser. High rise, single pleat, straight wide leg, 32 cm hem. Same cloth and colourways as the men's pleated trouser.
The Wrap Skirt
L-185 Trouser. Midi length, true wrap with self tie, deep hem. Falls straight when standing, opens on the step.
The Belted Jacket
L-160 Chambray. Shawl collar, self belt, patch pockets, unlined. Reads as a robe coat on the terrace and a jacket in town.

Construction, trims and consumption

Shell
L-145 Shirting, 100% linen, 145 gsm, 148 cm usable width
Seams
French seams at side and underarm (A, D). Flat-felled yoke seam, 1 cm. Stitch density 11 SPI, Tex 40 core-spun thread, dyed to match
Collar
One-piece camp collar, 30 gsm fusible on under collar only, so the roll stays soft
Placket
Concealed, 3 cm wide, 5 buttons at 26 cm pitch from 20 cm below HPS
Buttons
Corozo 18L, bone colour on all colourways, cross-stitched with shank. 1 spare in the hem tape
Pocket
Patch, 12 x 13 cm, 2 cm hem, bar tacks at corners, tone-on-tone
Hem
2 cm double-fold, 7 cm side vents with 1 cm facing
Labels
Woven main label at back neck in Bone, care and composition label at left side seam. No external branding
Consumption
1.65 m per size M at 148 cm, marker efficiency 82%, fusible 0.08 m
Finish
Garment wash: enzyme, soft finish, tumble low. Expected shrinkage within 3% warp and weft, built into the pattern
